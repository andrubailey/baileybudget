# Baseline — pre-rebuild app (`14de90a`), 2026-09-30

What was already broken before the three agents' rebuild, so Phase 2 can tell a regression from a pre-existing problem. Item IDs match [../checklist.md](../checklist.md).

**How this was measured:** production build of `14de90a` against the QA seed (2,196 transactions over 25 months, both people logging; 11,293 at 5×), in the four-device matrix, Chromium with safe-area emulation plus a WebKit cross-check. Every ❌ below has an automated test unless it says "manual". Nothing here touched the real Supabase project.

**Ranking:** trust defects first (anything that loses, duplicates or misstates money), regardless of how rare. Then everything else by how often you'd hit it in a normal week. Likelihood is my estimate from how the household uses the app — two people logging daily, reconciling against statements, mostly on iPhones as an installed app.

## Part 1 — Trust: loses, duplicates or misstates money

### T1. This month's spending is three different numbers · DC-1, DC-2
**Likelihood: every day.** Any month with one uncategorized or backdated transaction (the seed month has two of each; your real data has more).
- **Now:** for September, the Overview says **$19,782.30** (by transaction date), Spending → Cash flow says **$19,922.30** (by budget period, so it includes a $140 fee dated Aug 29 but filed under September), and the category breakdown and Budget tab say **$19,507.30** (drops $275 of uncategorized spending). On the Spending page itself, the hero says **"−$9,047.30 left"** and the breakdown right below it says **"$8,632.30 over"**.
- **Should:** one definition of "spent this month", used everywhere. Whichever definition you pick, uncategorized spending is still spending.
- **Where:** `lib/queries.ts` — `getPeriodSummaryForRange` (Overview), `getPeriodSummary` (Spending cash flow), `getCategoryProgressForRange` (breakdown, Budget tab, hero), `getCategoryProgress(periodId)` (budget editor — by period, unlike the Budget tab). Screens: `/`, `/spending`, `/budget`, `/spending/budget`.
- **Test:** `data-correctness.spec` → "this month's spending is the same number everywhere", "one over-budget figure".

### T2. Credit-card purchases are shown as money coming in · DC-6
**Likelihood: every day** — every card purchase.
- **Now:** the $1,249 Apple Store charge on the Amex shows as **"+$1,249.00" in green** on Recent. Reconcile corrections on the card show the same way (+$18.20 green).
- **Should:** a purchase on a card reads as money out (−, neutral color), whichever way it's stored.
- **Where:** row sign/color in `app/(app)/transaction-row.tsx`. A charge on a debt account is stored as kind `income` (it increases what's owed) and the row renders stored kind, not meaning.
- **Test:** `data-correctness.spec` → "a card purchase is never shown as money coming in".

### T3. Purchases waiting for approval already count as spent · DC-7 — **needs your decision**
**Likelihood: every week** you use approvals.
- **Now:** the $1,899 bunk beds and $1,249 Apple Store purchase (both "Needs approval") are in every total. Household shows the full $1,899, and Apps & Software shows **$1,258.99 spent of $20** — nothing says that's pending.
- **Should:** your call. Either leave them out of "spent" until approved, or keep them in but mark that part of the total as pending. Right now they're silently counted.
- **Where:** nothing filters `pending_approval` in `lib/queries.ts`.
- **Test:** `data-correctness.spec` → `test.fixme(...)`. I'll turn it on once you decide.

### T4. A reconcile correction is counted as income or spending · DC-9
**Likelihood: every month, per account you reconcile** (more often while you're catching up statements).
- **Now:** the gap shown is right, and "Just fix the balance" lands exactly on the statement figure (DC-8 ✅). But the correction is saved as an ordinary uncategorized income or expense row:
  - checking, bank shows $123.45 more → **Monthly Income +$123.45**
  - checking, bank shows $60 less → **Monthly Expenses +$60.00**
  - card, you owe $18.20 more → **Monthly Expenses +$18.20**
  - card, you owe $41.07 less → no change (the only direction that behaves)

  Each correction is also uncategorized, so it adds one to the "N transactions need a category" nag on every screen.
- **Should:** a bookkeeping correction changes the balance and nothing else — not income, not spending, not the "needs a category" count.
- **Where:** `reconcileAccountBalance` in `app/actions.ts:127` inserts `kind: diff > 0 ? "income" : "expense"` with `category_id: null`. Nothing marks it as an adjustment, so every total picks it up.
- **Test:** `reconcile.spec` (4 directions).

### T5. A half-typed transaction is lost if iOS reloads the app · SC-2
**Likelihood: a few times a month.** iOS unloads a backgrounded PWA under memory pressure — after a long call, the camera, or a game. You come back, it reloads, and the amount is empty.
- **Now:** backgrounding by itself keeps the input (SC-1 ✅). A reload brings the amount back as "0" and the description empty.
- **Should:** the draft (type, amount, description, account, category) is saved on every keystroke and restored when the sheet reopens.
- **Where:** `app/(app)/add/mobile-add-sheet.tsx` keeps everything in React state only. (There is an uncommitted `add/add-draft.ts` in the working tree from the rebuild — Phase 2 will check it.)
- **Test:** `trust.spec` → "reload/eviction preserves a half-typed transaction".

### T6. You can't open the app with no signal · NW-4, NW-5
**Likelihood: weekly** — a store or basement with no signal, and the app wasn't already open.
- **Now:** if the app is already open, logging with no signal works. The transaction is kept, you see "No connection — saved, will send automatically", it sends by itself when signal returns, and it saves exactly once (NW-4 ✅). But **the installed app can't be opened with no signal at all.** There's no service worker, so you get the system's offline page and can't log anything; the transaction is only in your head. Rarer: opening `/add` by URL, then saving offline, leaves a blank white screen (the sheet's close calls `router.back()`; nothing in the UI links there today).
- **Should:** the app opens offline, from cache, with the Add sheet working, and shows that it's offline.
- **Where:** no service worker in `public/`. Queue: `lib/offline-queue.ts`, `app/(app)/offline-queue-banner.tsx`.
- **Test:** `network.spec` → "the installed app still opens with no signal" (❌), "logged with no signal…" (✅ — keeps it that way).

### T7. A retry after a dropped connection creates a duplicate · NW-1, NW-2
**Likelihood: about once a month on cellular.** Rare on Wi-Fi, but permanent when it happens.
- **Now:** if the save reaches the server but the reply is lost (weak signal, elevator), the app shows **"Couldn't save: TypeError: fetch failed"**. You tap Add again and get **two transactions**. The offline queue does the same thing on its own: a save that actually landed but looked failed is queued, then replayed → **two rows**.
- **Should:** every save carries an ID created when the sheet opens. The server inserts it once and treats a repeat as "already saved". The message says "Couldn't confirm the save — checking…", not a raw error.
- **Where:** `mobile-add-sheet.tsx` `handleSubmit` creates a `pendingId` but never sends it to the server. `createTransaction` / `createTransfer` in `app/actions.ts` have no idempotency key, and `transactions` has no unique client id.
- **Test:** `trust.spec` → "retry after drop-after-commit", "offline-queue replay after a lost reply". (Double-tapping Add is already handled — NW-3 ✅.)

### T8. "Unknown" shown as who added a transaction · TP-3
**Likelihood: occasionally** — only when you open an older or AI-Advisor-inserted row.
- **Now:** transaction details show `created_by_email || "Unknown"`. Rows where the email wasn't stored show "Unknown", even when `created_by` (the user id) is there. New rows are attributed correctly (TP-2 ✅).
- **Should:** fall back to the person matching `created_by`, and show "Unknown" only when both are missing.
- **Where:** `app/(app)/transaction-detail-modal.tsx:416`.
- **Test:** manual (the seed has six such rows). Phase 2 will automate it.

### T9. "Left to spend" goes deeply negative after a planned payment from savings · DC-13
**Likelihood: a few times a year** (quarterly tax, the roof).
- **Now:** paying a $15,250 tax bill from Tax Savings pushes the hero to **"−$9,047.30 left"** for the month, even though the money was set aside for exactly that.
- **Should:** payments from a sinking fund either stay out of "left to spend" or are called out ("includes $15,250 from Tax Savings").
- **Where:** Spending hero, `app/(app)/spending/page.tsx`. This is interpretive, so it's a product call — flagged because the caption is misleading.

**To verify in Phase 2:** a refund on the credit card (stored as `expense` on a debt account) appears to reduce neither spending nor the category. The seed has one (`meta.edge_ids`).

### Performance (PF-1…8)

Production build, small phone, CPU slowed 2×, 80ms backend latency, median of 3 cold loads. Full tables: `artifacts/perf-baseline-1x/perf.md`, `artifacts/perf-baseline-5x/perf.md`.

| | 1× (2,196 txns) | 5× (11,293 txns) |
|---|---|---|
| Server response (TTFB) | 13–39ms | **307–423ms on every screen** |
| Interactive, Wi-Fi | 52–108ms | 340–894ms |
| Interactive, Slow 4G | 568–760ms | 740–**2,509ms** (`/transactions`) |
| Largest paint, Slow 4G | **2.0–2.6s** on `/balances`, `/recent`, `/add` | up to 2.8s |
| `/transactions` DOM nodes | 5,449 (not virtualized) | **23,723**, 92ms blocking |
| Tab switch | 27–61ms | 40–63ms |
| Tap → paint | 32–40ms | — |
| Layout shift on load | 0.000 everywhere | — |

**What degrades, and why:** the app caches each whole table, and Next.js won't cache anything over 2MB. At 5× the transactions table is 7.76MB, so it's never cached. Every page load re-downloads the entire table — 12 requests and 6.9MB just to open `/add`. At ~625 bytes a row, the cliff is **about 2,800–3,300 transactions, roughly three years at the seed's pace**. Your real data may be close. To check: run `select count(*) from transactions;` in the Supabase SQL editor, and look for `items over 2MB can not be cached` in the Vercel logs.

## What works today (don't regress these)

- Every account balance matches an independent ledger. Net worth = cash + equity. The Budget tab headline = the sum of its rows.
- Double-tapping Add on a slow connection saves once.
- Backgrounding the app keeps a half-typed transaction.
- A transaction logged offline (app already open) is kept, confirmed on screen, and sent by itself exactly once when signal returns.
- Reconcile's gap and "Just fix the balance" are arithmetically right.
- New transactions are attributed to the right person.
- Scroll position survives opening and closing a transaction.
- No float noise in any amount on 8 screens. No layout shift on load. Taps paint in under 40ms.
- Nothing readable sits under the status bar.
- Fixed pixel offsets for the keyboard: none. There's no keyboard handling at all (item 1), which is the actual defect.
